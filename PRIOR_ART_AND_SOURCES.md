# HOLDERCARD — Public prior-art reference and source chronology

**Published 3 November 2026.** Historical filing dates below are distinct from this publication date.

## Technical and advertising disclosure

HOLDERCARD is a flat, wallet-sized, one-piece foldable PVC physical advertising medium that converts into a smartphone stand. A front-facing branded lip is intended to remain visible in front of the supported smartphone. The stand makes the advertisement useful and encourages retention and repeated use. This is a functional and commercial concept, not a claim of independently proven consumer behavior.
